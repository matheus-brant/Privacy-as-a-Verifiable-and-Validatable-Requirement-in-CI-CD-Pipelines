# Stage 1 — Tertiary study

1. Define objectives, PICOC, MRQ and SRQs, search terms and database-specific strings.
2. Search ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, MDPI, ScienceDirect, Scopus, SpringerLink and Wiley Online Library for January 2014–December 2025.
3. Preserve the retrieval dates (18 February 2025 and 23 March 2026), BibTeX exports and source counts where sharing is permitted.
4. Import bibliographic records into Parsifal; identify duplicates and apply eligibility criteria.
5. Apply the five quality questions with scores 1/0.5/0, cutoff 3/5, with three reviewers; assess initial agreement with ordinal Krippendorff alpha and resolve disagreements in discussion.
6. Extract evidence aligned to the research questions; synthesize and document limitations.

The protocol workbook is in `protocol/`. The operational guide is in `procedures/`. Figures in `screenshots/` illustrate interfaces, not a full machine-readable Parsifal export. Exact database-specific search strings and retrieval records must be preserved to support full re-execution.
